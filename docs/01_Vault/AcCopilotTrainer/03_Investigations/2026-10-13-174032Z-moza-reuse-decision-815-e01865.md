## Reuse decision

Use our existing native recorder first for practice driven by MOZA. The observed 137.983 s
Porsche/Spa archive already contains the telemetry our code consumes; the MOZA practice cache
mostly corroborates those channels. Add explicit AI-control attribution and consumer checks
before claiming human progress or selecting a teaching reference. Raw bytes stay immutable.

The cache adapter remains useful for incremental inputs: separate pre-recorded comparisons and,
when actually available, evaluated advice. Keep those roles separate from the operator's practice
record. The captured practice cache directly joins MOZA evaluation id/recordUid and is marked
AI-intervened / ai-debug-only. Its native association is prospective timing/combo/trajectory
evidence, not a shared native/MOZA session ID. Exclusive uninterrupted control is not claimed.

The separate UI-labelled AI Best is 138.345 s, 0.362 s slower than the observed AI practice lap.
Its label therefore proves neither a faster target nor suitable human technique. It could still
help a separately qualified human baseline; that comparison was not performed. MOZA's own
[guide](https://support.mozaracing.com/en/support/solutions/articles/70000683725-what-is-racing-lab-how-do-i-use-it-)
describes reinforcement-learning references without human demonstration data and potentially
unconventional inputs. The observed launch configured stability control 100; conditions matter.
